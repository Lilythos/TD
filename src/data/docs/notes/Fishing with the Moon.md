# Fishing with the Moon

I cast my fishing line,
Yet the moon clung fast
To the silver-glinting hook.

So I fish with the moon instead.

I smooth my hair again and again,
Hearing only the pine trees murmur low,
And birds exchange soft whispers of love.

Where are you, my fish?

My little cat
Must be home already,
Meowing to call me back.

Slowly, the moon
Slips off the hook,
And drifts away from my eyes.

No fish has come.
Yet my heart has wandered off with the moon.
